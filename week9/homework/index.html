Assignment

Start by creating a visual pattern in P5js sketch. Develop it into a rhythmic composition that displays variation across the entire window.

  • Use HSB color mode to demonstrate the variations of color.
  • Use loops to iterate through variations.
  • Pay attention to the composition, tiling and symmetry.

My Process

For this project, my first thought was to create a wall of shapes that replicate fish scales. That was a little too ambitious at the moment given my skill and the time available, so I decided that I would stick with an array of diamonds that could be interacted with while I learn to read p5.js.

Had a hard time understanding how to rotate the grid 45°. I kept trying to build an array of squares and then rotate the entire thing. But this caused me to either rotate everything in the canvas, creating a giant diamond of diamonds, or rotate the canvas itself. Neither of those were the desired outcome.

I continued looking for a rotate() function, thinking that QUARTER_PI was for something more complicated than what I was working on. But after finally giving in and reading through the p5.js documentation I realized that it was exactly what I needed to rotate each item individually within the array. It also would have allowed me to target a specific item and rotate ONLY that item, but that's not what I was after. Just something good to know for later.

Attempt 1

I wanted to round the corners of the diamonds generated by the rotated grid. But how do you round the corners of a shape that is only passively defined by another shape? In this case, the lines? I had to re-think this approach.

Attempt 2

The original code used lines to create the diamond shapes. I decided to instead create an array of actual squares, each rotated 45° instead of rotating the entire canvas. Then they are made to fill the container. Then I curved each corner with quadraticVertex(). The diamonds sit in the same places as before.

Attempt 3

I wanted to add a click event to the squares, so that clicking a square turned it a random color, then caused a ripple effect where that color was duplicated but faded as the distance from the original click target increased. At first, I specifically defined the timing and color changes I wanted. However, the LLM interpreted this too literally and I ended up with a click event that felt unnatural and forced.

Click a diamond to activate pretty cool disco mode!

Attempt 4 [FINAL]

In this final step, I allowed the LLM to determine a timing that felt more natural so that it created more of a circular feeling "ripple" of random color that traveled away from the click target.

Click a diamond to activate EVEN COOLER disco mode!

Note that if you click fast enough, you can trigger the event to be active at more than one origin point at the same time.
